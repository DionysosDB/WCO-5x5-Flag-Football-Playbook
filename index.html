H takes the direct snap while QB lines up wide-left acting as a receiver. At snap, H immediately laterals backward to QB who has dropped into the backfield. QB Read: Line up wide-left → drop into backfield at snap → receive lateral from H → sweep right OR throw back to H.

🏆 Receiver Fundamentals

⚡ Run FULL SPEED to your break point — never slow down early
⚡ Plant hard on your outside foot before breaking inside, vice versa
⚡ Sell every route like it's a deep ball — beat the defender first
⚡ After your cut, eyes to QB immediately for the ball
⚡ Catch with your hands, not your body — soft hands win games!

Every defender owns exactly one receiver man-to-man — no zones, no help. Four CBs line up at the line of scrimmage directly across from their assigned player (X, H, Center, Z). At snap each CB follows their man everywhere. Rusher starts at the 7-yd rush line at a slight angle from the QB so they can navigate around the Center and pull the QB’s flag before the throw.
⚠ Pick and rub routes (Mesh, Lion) are deadly — defenders get screened by each other. If offense keeps running crossing combos, switch to Cover 1 for the safety help.

Three CBs lock man-to-man on X, H, Z — each lined up 2–3 yds off the LOS. Free Safety drops 10+ yards deep in the center zone, eliminating slants, posts and fly routes from hitting. Rusher attacks the QB from the 7-yd rush line on snap. The Center is not man-covered — the FS zone absorbs anything thrown into the deep middle.
⚠ Vulnerable to Smash: CB sits on the hitch underneath while WR2 runs a corner over the Safety. Rotate to Cover 2 if they keep hitting it.

⚠ Vulnerable to Smash (hitch underneath + corner over safety) and seam routes splitting the two safeties. Switch to Cover 1 if they keep hitting corner routes.

No rusher — all five defenders in zone. Two mid-zone defenders start 5 yds off LOS and drop back to cover the 5–10 yd window, watching for slants, in routes and out routes. Three safeties guard one third of the deep field each (left third, middle third, right third) from the 10-yd line to the end zone. Safeties must keep all receivers in front of them at all times.
⚠ Short crossing routes (Mesh, Dragon) and flat routes can be open underneath. Accept short completions — prevent the big play and first down at all costs.

Four defenders line up 10 yards back from the LOS, each guarding one quarter of the deep field from the 10-yard mark to the end zone. The rusher crosses the LOS on snap to pressure the QB — but can fall back to help cover short middle routes if needed.
